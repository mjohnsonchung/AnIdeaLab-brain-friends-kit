The owner's second brain: AI agents working on their projects, and everything they know. Its rules are the core, [CLAUDE.md](../../../CLAUDE.md). It's a core, a skills library and a knowledge library.

It was started on {KIT_DATE} from the AnIdeaLab brain kit: the core, both libraries, the cards and indexes, the scripts, the Obsidian settings and the brain graph, and a GitHub Actions runner on the owner's PC, with empty shelves, 64 skills, and guide mode on, because the owner is new to AI agents.
