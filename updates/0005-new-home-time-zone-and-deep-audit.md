# Brain update 5 from AnIdeaLab: the kit's new home, reminders in your own time zone, a monthly deep audit, stale tasks cleared, a key script for any key, and lessons folded into every skill

Sent 5 Nov 2026. The kit your brain came from now has a home of its own, AnIdeaLab's repo [AnIdeaLab/brain-kit](https://github.com/AnIdeaLab/brain-kit), and this update moves your brain over: from now on it looks for updates there. It also brings what came out of the first reports on update 4 and one brain's weekly tidy-up: a monthly deep audit if you'd like one, stale tasks closed in the tidy-up, the key script for any service, lessons folded into every skill, an up-to-date skill-creator, reminders in your own time zone, a shorter brain graph panel, and the kit's notes in its own words. It needs update 4 first: a brain still on update 3 applies update 4, then this one.

## Summary for the owner
- **Updates come from AnIdeaLab's own repo now:** the kit moved to AnIdeaLab/brain-kit, where it stands on its own, and your brain will check there for updates from now on. Nothing else about updates changes: you still hear what each one does, and it's applied only if you say go. The kit's notes and skills now give their reasons in the kit's own words.
- **Reminders in your own time:** reminders, like the weekly tidy-up, were read in New Zealand time. Now they use your time zone. Your agent will ask you once where you live.
- **A monthly deep audit, if you'd like it:** once a month, your agent reads every decision in your brain in full to find rules that clash, and asks a fresh agent questions about your brain to find notes that mislead it, then fixes them. It uses more of your plan than the weekly tidy-up, so like the tidy-up it runs only when you say yes; your agent will ask once whether you want it.
- **Old tasks get cleared:** a task only closed when the agent working on it closed it, so finished and forgotten ones piled up (one brain had 81 open at one point). Now the weekly tidy-up looks at every task that hasn't moved in two weeks, closes the ones that are done or overtaken, and moves finished ones into a history folder, so your tasks list shows only what's still to do. Anything of yours it can't settle, it asks you about as "keep or drop?".
- **Adding a key works for any service:** the script your agent gives you for putting a key into your brain's GitHub secrets used to know only a few services by name. Now it knows none: your agent's command says which key, the page that makes it and what to press, so it works the same for anything. It's still one command pasted into PowerShell.
- **Lessons go into every skill:** when a skill lets your agent down, it writes a lesson beside the skill, and the weekly tidy-up folds those lessons into the skill itself. Until now it did that only for the skills your brain made. Now it does it for every skill, so each one gets better from your own use.
- **The skill for making skills is up to date:** skill-creator is now Anthropic's newer version. Some brains had already taken it; they'll find nothing changes.
- **A shorter brain graph panel:** the brain graph's options are now four short cards (Replay, Links, Bubbles, Look), with three more accent colours, and the replay always shows the whole history.
- **Design samples:** when a question is about how something should look, your agent can have two fresh agents each make a design from the same brief, for you to compare.
- **Reading an update in full:** agents now save everything an update prints and read all of it, since the places where your changes and the kit's met come first.

Nothing runs on your PC, and there's nothing to paste.

## What changed, and why
1. **The kit's new home.** `skills/brain-spec/brain_update.py` reads updates from [AnIdeaLab/brain-kit](https://github.com/AnIdeaLab/brain-kit), and when its local copy of the kit (in `~/.cache/brain-updates`) came from somewhere else, it clones it again from there. Why: the kit now lives in AnIdeaLab's own repo, apart from any one person's brain, and every update after this one comes from there; the kit's earlier home sends no more. This update is also in the new repo, as its update 5, with `brain/` exactly as here, so the next update merges from the same starting point and `compare` holds your brain to the same exemplar. The one-line installer from the earlier home now runs the new repo's installer, so an old link still works.
2. **Reminders in the owner's own time zone.** `skills/brain-spec/reminders.py`, `knowledge/systems/routines.md` (a "**Time zone:**" line under "The owner's own", rule 1, and both tables' When column, now "When (the owner's time)") and `skills/getting-started/SKILL.md` (it asks a new owner where they live and fills the line in). Why: `reminders.py` read every reminder's time as New Zealand time, which suits owners there but not anyone else. Now it reads the line; until it's set, or if Python can't read the zone, it reads times in the computer's own time zone (UTC in a cloud session) and tells the agent to ask the owner.
3. **The monthly deep audit.** New: `skills/brain-audit/deep-audit.md`, `skills/brain-audit/quiz.json` (empty: each deep audit adds its own questions) and `knowledge/areas/the-brain/deep-audit-log.md`. Changed: `skills/brain-audit/SKILL.md` (its description, and a line on the two levels), `skills/getting-started/SKILL.md` (part 6 offers it too), `skills/lists/the-brain.md`, `knowledge/areas/the-brain/README.md`, `knowledge/areas/README.md` and the example rows in `knowledge/systems/routines.md`. Why: a weekly check of scripts and cards misses rules that clash without linking to each other, and removed rules surviving in other words; only reading the notes in full catches those.
4. **The add-a-key script is for any key.** `skills/add-a-key/add-api-keys.ps1` and `skills/add-a-key/SKILL.md`. The script had a table of services, and every new service needed a change to it. Now it has none: the command gives `-Secret`, the secret's name, and if it helps `-Page`, `-Tell`, `-Prefix` and `-Check`. Keys already in your secrets stay as they are.
5. **The weekly tidy-up folds every skill's lessons in.** `skills/brain-audit/SKILL.md` (part 3), `skills/brain-spec/SKILL.md` ("How skills learn") and `skills/brain-spec/library.py` (`audit` now says "fold in" for every skill with a `LESSONS.md`; its test changed with it). Before, the kit's own skills and copied ones kept their lessons apart in `LESSONS.md`, and one brain's tidy-up found the script and the skill disagreeing about it. Why it's safe: an update merges its changes around lines a brain added, and where they meet, the brain spec now counts a folded lesson as the owner's own, to put back.
6. **The owner's own stuff now includes folded lessons.** `skills/brain-spec/SKILL.md`, "Brain updates": lessons the tidy-up folded into a skill join the list of things an update keeps, so this change can't be undone by the next update.
7. **skill-creator brought up to date** (7 files in `skills/skill-creator/`), from Anthropic's newer copy, the one Claude sessions carry. Why: brains that had taken it already saw `compare` list it as changed every time; with the kit on the same copy, that goes.
8. **The tidy-up closes stale tasks.** `skills/brain-audit/SKILL.md` (its description, and a new Part 1 step 6, "Stale tasks"; the steps after it move down one), `knowledge/tasks/README.md` (finished tasks stay two weeks, then move to `history/`) and `skills/brain-spec/SKILL.md` ("When a task closes"). Why: a task closes only when the agent working on it closes it, so they pile up; one brain had 81 unfinished, 34 unchanged in over a week.
9. **Read all of what `apply` prints.** `skills/brain-spec/SKILL.md`, "Brain updates": save `apply`'s output to a file and read it whole. Why: a report on update 4 said its agent piped the output through `tail`, and the clashes, which print first, scrolled away.
10. **Design directions.** `skills/brain-spec/SKILL.md` ("Handing work to an agent"), `skills/guide-mode/SKILL.md` (step 2) and the starting rules: a question about how something should look or be laid out can be answered with samples, two fresh agents each making a design from the same brief, which the owner compares at a check-in. They count within one or two agents at a time. Why: designs made apart, from the same brief, give the owner fresh takes rather than variations on one agent's first idea.
11. **The brain graph's options panel, trimmed.** `.obsidian/plugins/brain-graph/main.js` and `styles.css`, `.obsidian/plugins/brain-graph/data.json` and `knowledge/systems/brain-graph.md`. Start from, One at a time, Hub links, Fade old links, Hide excluded notes, Your edits in their own colour and Quieter bots go from the panel: the replay is the whole history, links to the hub notes are hidden, links don't fade, and the rest stay on. The panel is Replay, Links, Bubbles, then Look, as titled cards, with a Hold shape slider and three more accents (mint, amber, pink). Why: a short panel is easier to use, and the options that went were rarely changed. `data.json` holds the owner's settings, so where both changed it the owner's lines were kept; settings the graph no longer has are ignored.
12. **The kit in its own words.** Every mention of the person the kit was first made from, and of that person's brain, friends and ventures, is gone, and each rule's reason is given in the kit's own words, with AnIdeaLab where it named them. The core (`CLAUDE.md`: the defaults' opening line, "They came from the AnIdeaLab brain kit") and the front door (`README.md`); the starting rules (`knowledge/decisions/the-brain/<your brain's start date>-starting-rules.md`, now a plain list of each default and gate with its reason) and the decisions index; `knowledge/areas/the-brain/README.md`; `knowledge/ideas/friction-log.md` (its opening); in `knowledge/systems/`: `brain-graph.md`, `brain-updates/README.md`, `keys-and-accounts.md` (the testing account's row: AnIdeaLab's testing account), `obsidian-setup.md` and `sign-off-links.md`; the skills `README.md` and `lists/sources.md`; the skills add-a-key, brain-audit (`SKILL.md`, `deep-audit.md`), brain-spec, find-skills, getting-started, guide-mode, handoff (`SKILL.md`, `nudge.json`), my-pc, upload-point and windows-powershell; and the scripts `brain_update.py`, `check_links.py`, `library.py`, `move.py`, `reminders.py`, `skill_updates.py`, `test_library.py`, `nudge.py`, `check-setup.ps1`, `setup-runner.ps1`, `upload-point.ps1`, the workflows `pc-run.yml` and `update-report.yml`, the brain graph's `main.js` and test runner, and `.obsidian/snippets/brain-colours.css`.
