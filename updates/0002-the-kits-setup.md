# Brain update 2 from AnIdeaLab: fixes, and the kit's setup

Sent 26 Oct 2026. Fixes from the first two reports on update 1, a shorter skills list in the shape Matt's own brain uses, a rule for what an update keeps of a brain's own, and fuller reports. It replaces an update 2 sent earlier the same day, which no brain had applied.

## Summary for the owner
Fixes from the first brains to get update 1, and a tidier setup:
- The skills list gets shorter: your brain's own skills stay on it, and the copied ones move into lists by topic. Agents read it at the start of most jobs, so this saves some of your usage.
- When an update and your brain changed the same lines, your agent now sees both sides. Your own notes, skills and settings stay; old fixes your brain made to the kit's own parts give way to the kit's.
- Your Obsidian settings, like the brain graph's speed, stay as you set them.
- The link check no longer counts copied skills as your brain's own.
- The report your brain sends AnIdeaLab after an update says more about how it went. It still leaves out your notes, projects and people.

Nothing runs on your PC.

## What changed, and why
- **The skills list** (`skills/README.md`) now lists the brain's own skills, and links seven lists by topic in `skills/lists/` (planning, code, websites and design, AI and Claude, Obsidian, business, life admin), which hold the copied skills, one line each. Why: every agent reads the skills list at the start of a job, and the one-page list of all 60 skills had grown to 9.5 KB; this one is under 5 KB. Matt's own brain moved to this shape on 22 Oct 2026, when its list got too long, and his brain has shown it works. The brain spec and find-skills now say a new copied skill goes in a topic list, never on the skills list itself, and `library.py check` flags the skills list past 6 KB, so it stays short.
- **What an update keeps of a brain's own** (the brain spec's "Brain updates" section). Matt, 26 Oct 2026: "I feel like their brains are set up in the right way now. I don't want to let any changes that originally happened with them messing around with it in its early stages to carry through now that we have the right update applied to it." So an update brings a brain to the kit as it is now. The owner's own stuff stays: their notes, skills and lists they added, their own tools, their PC's folders, their Obsidian settings. A brain's own earlier fix or workaround for a kit part gives way to the kit's fix. Why: a brain that keeps its own fixes drifts from the kit, every drift is a clash in a later update that costs the owner's usage and leaves a judgement call to whichever agent applies it, and Matt tries each update on a brain like the kit, so a drifted brain gets one nobody tried.
- **Clashes show both sides** (`skills/brain-spec/brain_update.py`). Where the brain and an update changed the same lines, the update's lines go in, as before. But git takes the whole clashing stretch, so in update 1 a paragraph of a brain's own, sitting next to a sentence the update changed, went too, and only an agent reading the diff saw it (both reports). From the next update on, `apply` prints the brain's side and the update's side of each clash, with the rule above beside it.
- **Obsidian's settings stay the owner's** (`brain_update.py`). In `.obsidian/` settings files (the JSON ones, but not a plugin's `manifest.json`), where both changed the same lines, the brain's own lines stay. In update 1 a brain's graph speed, set by its owner, was put back to the kit's.
- **Fuller reports** (`brain_update.py`, the brain spec). The script now writes down which update came after which, the brain's commit before it, every file and what happened to it, and how big each clash was. The agent fills in six sections: the owner's answer, the message's steps, the clashes, what broke or puzzled it, code it changed, and the job's size. A new step, `brain_update.py finish`, runs the brain's checks, adds their results, and won't finish while a section is empty. Why: Matt reads each report without seeing the brain, and the first ones said less than he needed. Problems the checks find in the owner's own notes are counted, not named.
- **The brain-updates note gets today's date** when an update is applied or skipped. It kept the brain's start date.
- **The link check** (`skills/brain-spec/check_links.py`): a list linked under "Copied skills" stays a list of copied skills, even when "The brain's own" links it too. In one brain that made every skill in a list count as its own, and the check reported 32 broken links and 157 unlinked notes inside copied skills.
- **The brain's copy on the PC** (`skills/my-pc/SKILL.md`): the commands that name `C:\AI-Brain-Link\<repo name>` now say a brain cloned somewhere else uses the folder in its PC note.
